Clause 2.0

Product Safety & Supervision Disclaimers

While FURBABY strives to stock only high-quality, pet-safe, non-toxic items for USA dog owners, pet behavior varies widely. Please read the specific category notices below:

Dog Neck Collars & Leashes

Collars must be fitted with a minimum 2-finger margin between the collar and your dog's neck. Never tie up your dog unattended while wearing a training or choke collar. Regular inspection for wear, tear, or fraying on buckles and D-rings is the pet owner's sole responsibility.

Dog Apparel & Clothing

Apparel items (sweaters, coats, hoodies) should not restrict your dog's natural movement, breathing, or vision. Do not leave clothing on dogs in hot weather to prevent heatstroke. Immediately remove clothing if your dog shows signs of anxiety or chewing on fabric.

Stainless & Silicone Feeding Bowls

All feeding bowls and slow feeders sold by FURBABY are BPA-free and food-grade safe. Inspect bowls regularly for deep scratches or cracks that could harbor bacteria or cause tongue injury. Clean stainless steel and silicone bowls daily.

Rubber & Chew Toys

No dog toy is 100% indestructible. Always supervise your dog during playtime. If a rubber chew toy or plush squeaker becomes torn or broken into small swallowable pieces, take it away immediately to prevent intestinal blockage or choking hazards.

Dog Safety Checklist for Owners

Verify these safety points before giving new collars or toys to your dog

  • Can you easily slide 2 fingers between your dog’s neck and collar?
  • Is the metal D-ring tightly secured before attaching a leash?
  • Are you removing collars during crate time to prevent accidental snagging?
  • Are there any loose, torn, or half-chewed rubber fragments?
  • Is the toy size appropriate so your dog cannot swallow it whole?
  • Are you monitoring play sessions for aggressive power chewers?
  • Is the feeder placed on a non-slip rubber mat to avoid tipping?
  • Are food bowls washed after every meal with pet-safe soap?
  • Are slow-feeder mazes clear of cracks or sharp plastic wear?
